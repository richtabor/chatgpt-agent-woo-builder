# Interval Product Direction

Interval serves run-club regulars, race-block runners, and gift buyers shopping for someone deep in training.

Assortment rules:

- Keep the catalog curated instead of warehouse-like.
- Merchandise by scenario and ritual, not only by department.
- Balance apparel, carry and hydration, recovery, and race-day prep.
- Use expert-but-welcoming copy that sounds like a specialist outfitter.

Initial storefront emphasis:

- Humid dawn systems
- Cold rain miles
- Track-night visibility and carry
- Long-run fueling
- Race-week organization
- Recovery between city sessions
