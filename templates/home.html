How a jar happens

Three steps. One jar. A pantry that earns its rent.

Pick a starter system. Set it on the counter. Wait, then taste. Brine ships everything as a kit so the only thing you have to remember is to taste a spoonful at day three.

01

Pick a starter

A 1L crock, a culture pouch, sea salt, and a pamphlet. Pick by outcome — kraut, hot sauce, miso — not by science.

02

Set it on the counter

Out of direct sun, off the radiator. The vessel does the work; you walk past it twice a day and feel quietly accomplished.

03

Taste at day three

Spoon, sniff, decide. Most jars want another week; some want the fridge tonight. We text you reminders if you want them.
